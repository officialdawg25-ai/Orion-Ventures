# Customer Acquisition Engine

The acquisition module turns customer growth into a traceable workflow rather than a collection of unconnected social posts or ad-hoc follow-ups.

## Core path

1. **Target segment** — define who has the problem, where they can be reached, eligibility and exclusions.
2. **Offer** — define the outcome, scope, price/terms, proof, delivery capacity and expiration.
3. **Experiment or campaign** — define hypothesis, channel, budget/time cap, attribution method and success/stop criteria.
4. **Prospect** — store only necessary contact details, lawful basis/permission where applicable, source, assigned owner and next action.
5. **Qualification** — record observed need, fit, timing, budget/ability to buy where appropriate, and disqualification reason.
6. **Follow-up** — time-stamped activity, next due date, responsible owner and contact preference.
7. **Conversion** — link to the actual sale/contract/booking record; do not count a click or verbal promise as revenue.
8. **Onboarding and delivery** — record handoff, first-value milestone, service outcome and issues.
9. **Retention and referral** — track repeat purchase/cohort behavior and consented referrals.

## Required measurement definitions

- Lead source and campaign ID.
- Reporting period and timezone.
- Count definition for each funnel stage.
- Conversion numerator and denominator.
- Spend definition (cash spend and allocated labor shown separately when available).
- Acquisition cost formula and exclusions.
- Attribution model and known limitations.
- Refund/cancellation treatment.
- Data freshness and source links.

## Rules

- Never invent conversion rates, customer counts or testimonials.
- Keep experiments small enough to be affordable and interpretable.
- Separate organic attention from qualified intent and paid conversion.
- Deduplicate contacts conservatively and retain merge history.
- Respect opt-outs and applicable privacy/marketing rules.
- Do not send messages automatically until provider, consent, rate limits, approval and audit behavior are implemented.
- Use human review for material budget changes and public claims.
