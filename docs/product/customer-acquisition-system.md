# Customer acquisition system

**Status:** PROPOSED.

## Funnel
Target segment → problem hypothesis → offer → channel experiment → prospect → qualified lead → follow-up → conversion → onboarding → retention → referral.

## Required records
Segment/customer hypothesis; offer, price and exclusions; channel/campaign and experiment window; lead source and contact permissions; contact history and next action; outcome and conversion date; revenue and attributable costs; retention, repeat purchase, cancellation and referral signals; experiment owner, budget, hypothesis and stop/continue criteria.

## Metrics
Lead-to-qualified, qualified-to-customer, time to conversion, revenue by channel, customer acquisition cost where costs exist, repeat purchase/retention, refunds/cancellations and contribution after variable costs. Show sample size, period, currency, attribution method and missing data. Correlation is not causal lift.

## Playbooks
Customer interviews, local partnerships, referrals, landing-page tests, direct sales, content experiments, post-purchase follow-up, win-back and churn review. Each playbook defines purpose, audience, steps, approvals, measurement and stop condition.

## Compliance
Respect applicable POPIA requirements, marketing preferences, opt-outs, access controls and retention. Do not scrape personal contact details or send campaigns without authority. AI may draft within approved data; campaign launch and spend remain human-approved.
