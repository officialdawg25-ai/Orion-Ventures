# Venture lifecycle and stage gates

**Status:** PROPOSED.

## Stages
1. **Idea captured:** problem, target customer, thesis, assumptions and constraints.
2. **Validation:** interviews, competitor evidence, demand tests, willingness-to-pay signals and a bounded experiment.
3. **Business case:** offer, channel hypothesis, operating model, budget, unit economics, risks and compliance review.
4. **Launch:** initial offer, delivery process, onboarding, support and measurement.
5. **Stabilization:** repeatable fulfillment, customer satisfaction, cost visibility and basic controls.
6. **Growth:** validated acquisition, retention, contribution margin, capacity and funding plan.
7. **Restructure / pause / exit:** rationale, obligations, customer transition, access revocation and records retention.

## Gate record
Every transition records current/target stage, required evidence, source/date, decision owner, timestamp, budget requested/approved, risk assessment, criteria met/not met, exceptions, next review and rationale.

## Example criteria
- Validation: target-customer evidence, tested problem statement and falsifiable demand hypothesis.
- Launch: viable offer, operational readiness, support path, pricing and applicable legal/industry checks.
- Growth: repeated conversion, known acquisition costs, retention signals, capacity and cash forecast.
- Exit: customer/vendor/employee obligations reviewed; professional advice obtained where needed; access safely revoked and records retained per policy.

Thresholds vary by venture type and stage. Do not fund, pause or close a venture solely on an opaque score or AI recommendation.
