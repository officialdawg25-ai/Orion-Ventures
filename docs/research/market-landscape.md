# Market landscape and positioning

**Status:** PROPOSED research synthesis; prices must be rechecked before procurement. **Reviewed:** 2026-10-26.

## Product thesis
MPOS coordinates portfolio-level decisions and daily execution. It should not replace every CRM, accounting package, project manager, HR system and document platform in Release 1. Its differentiator is connecting venture lifecycle evidence, owner attention, cash commitments, customer acquisition and portfolio allocation.

## Categories to benchmark
- Knowledge/workspaces: Notion.
- Configurable data and automation: Airtable.
- Tasks and workload: ClickUp.
- Integrated business applications: Odoo and Zoho.
- Accounting: Xero South Africa and QuickBooks South Africa.
- Analytics: Power BI.
- Venture studios: Venture Capital Studio, University of Melbourne MEC Venture Studio and South African operators such as Robotiq.

## Initial comparison
| Category | Common strength | MPOS response |
|---|---|---|
| Knowledge | Pages and shared workspaces | Connect evidence, decisions, tasks and venture records |
| Project execution | Tasks, due dates and workload views | Tie work to portfolio priorities and stage gates |
| Accounting | Reconciliation, invoicing and tax reports | Start with management visibility and integrate specialist accounting |
| CRM | Lead pipelines and communications | Link experiments and conversion economics to venture stage |
| Analytics | Dashboards and visualizations | Explain each metric and trace it to source records |
| Venture building | Validation, launch and shared services | Codify repeatable stage gates and playbooks |
| AI | Summaries and automation | Require provenance, uncertainty, permissions and human approval |

## Research implications
- Gate investment behind customer and operating evidence.
- Review capital and owner attention regularly.
- Reuse playbooks while preserving legal, financial and access boundaries.
- Make data portability and integration a first-class requirement.
- Pricing is region-, billing-period- and plan-dependent; recheck current terms.
