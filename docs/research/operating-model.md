# Portfolio operating model

**Status:** PROPOSED.

## Principles
1. Evidence before investment.
2. One portfolio review cadence with venture-specific operating rhythms.
3. Keep legal entities and books distinct where the real structure requires it.
4. Every priority has an owner, outcome, due date and review date.
5. Capital and owner attention are scarce resources.
6. Pause and closure are valid outcomes.
7. Preserve decision rationale and lessons learned.

## Review cadence
Run a monthly operating review and a quarterly allocation review, adjusted for portfolio size and risk. Review stage, customer traction, revenue quality, margin/unit economics, cash runway and commitments, risks, delivery capacity, owner time, milestone evidence and next decision.

Possible actions: **grow, maintain, repair, validate, pause, restructure, exit/close**. These are decision options, not automated judgments.

## Capital allocation
For each proposed spend, record amount, currency, purpose, expected outcome, alternatives, downside, milestone, approval authority and required evidence. Prefer small reversible experiments before large commitments where appropriate. Avoid one opaque score; show evidence dimensions and trade-offs.

## Shared services
Maintain reusable playbooks for customer discovery, sales, marketing, finance operations, document control, hiring, vendor selection, security and launch checklists. Reuse procedures, not private customer data or records across unrelated ventures.

## Owner attention
Track planned versus actual effort at useful granularity without intrusive surveillance. Highlight overload and bottlenecks; do not infer productivity from activity counts alone.

## Legal boundary
A portfolio grouping is not automatically a holding company. Store legal ownership, entity relationships, intercompany balances and authority separately from informal venture grouping. Obtain qualified South African legal and accounting advice for entity, tax, employment and governance decisions.
